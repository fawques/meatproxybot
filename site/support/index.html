Frequently Asked Questions

Does the bot reveal who triggered it?

No. The callout response never mentions who used the reaction, slash command, or shortcut. The bot only shows the poster's name (who wrote the original message).

Can I use Meatproxy in my direct messages?

No. Meatproxy only works in channels where it has been installed. You cannot use it in direct messages or group DMs.

What happens if I uninstall the app?

Your workspace's installation data is deleted immediately. Audit logs are deleted within 30 days as part of normal log rotation.

How can I request a copy of my data?

Contact us at SUPPORT_EMAIL with "data request" in the subject line. We will provide all data associated with your workspace within one month.

Does the bot save message text?

No. The bot never logs or saves message text. Audit logs only record metadata (who triggered it, when, where, and the result).

Can the bot be used to harass someone?

While the bot itself is designed to be lighthearted, it could theoretically be misused. If you believe someone is using Meatproxy to harass you, contact your workspace's Owners or Admins, or reach out to us at SUPPORT_EMAIL.

Is Meatproxy free?

Yes. Meatproxy is a free Slack app.
